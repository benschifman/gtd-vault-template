How long state and local permitting decisions take, how that is measured, and what drives it.

## Body 
   <!-- Wikipedia-style article that discusses the topic in context to other related topics, including how they relate to each other, why they are important, why they are notable, what they are about, etc. -->
Decision time is the most cited and least consistently measured permitting metric. The Cityworks dataset is the best current source; its main limitation is coverage of jurisdictions that publish decision dates. The policy question is whether statutory deadlines change outcomes or only shift where the delay appears.
