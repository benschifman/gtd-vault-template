Reform of the laws and processes that govern approval of housing and infrastructure projects, at state and federal level.

## Body 
   <!-- Wikipedia-style article that discusses the topic in context to other related topics, including how they relate to each other, why they are important, why they are notable, what they are about, etc. -->
Permitting reform covers changes to the statutes, regulations, and administrative practice that determine how long it takes to approve a project and who can stop it. At the state level the live questions are shot clocks, by-right approval, and staffing; at the federal level they are environmental review scope and the coordination of overlapping agency processes.
