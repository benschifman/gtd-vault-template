Who has authority to approve the route of an electricity transmission line, and how those authorities overlap.

## Body 
   <!-- Wikipedia-style article that discusses the topic in context to other related topics, including how they relate to each other, why they are important, why they are notable, what they are about, etc. -->
Interstate transmission lines typically require approval from each state they cross, plus federal approvals for land crossings, with FERC holding a limited backstop. The result is three overlapping processes with no single decision-maker.
